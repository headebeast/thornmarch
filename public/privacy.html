Privacy notice

Your privacy, plainly

The game is free, has no ads and no account. If — and only if — you say yes, it records anonymous usage statistics so the game can be made better. This page lists exactly what that means.

In one breath: nothing is collected until you press Allow. You can switch it off any time in Settings, and you can erase what was recorded about your browser with one button. Your name, your chat and your moves are never recorded.

What is recorded (only after you press Allow)

  • Where in the game you are, and for how long — e.g. "main menu, 40 seconds", "Battle Royale, 12 minutes".
  • Which buttons you press — by the name of the button ("Battle Royale", "Recruit…"), never by what you typed.
  • What you play — which mode, how many players, how long the match lasted, whether you won, lost or left.
  • How long you really use the page — time with the page visible and in use; and how many minutes you play.
  • Scrolling and zooming — how many times, and how far a list was scrolled.
  • How smoothly the game runs — average frame rate; and, in online rooms, average ping and packet loss.
  • Crashes — the error message and file name, to fix bugs.
  • Your device, roughly — phone/tablet/desktop, operating system and browser family, screen size, language, time zone, and which website sent you here.
  • Your IP address and approximate location. Your IP address reaches the server with every request. It is used to work out your country, region and city (never your street) and to tell visitors apart. By default the last part of the address is removed before anything is saved (203.0.113.77 becomes 203.0.113.0), and a one-way scrambled version is kept only to recognise repeat visits. The site operator can choose to keep the whole address (for example to block abuse); if they do, this notice is updated.
  • A random visitor number, stored in your browser, so one person who comes back is not counted as five.

What is never recorded

Your name · anything you type, including chat and room names · the contents of your matches or your moves · where on the map you click · your exact location · the websites you visit · anything from other tabs or apps · your full user-agent string.

Why

To see which modes people enjoy and which they abandon, where the game is confusing, how well it runs on different devices, and where it crashes. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or used to build a profile of you.

Where it is kept, and for how long

On the site's hosting provider (Netlify), readable only by the site operator through a password-protected dashboard. Their servers may be in another country than yours. Each play session is deleted automatically 90 days after it happened.

Your choices

  • Say no. Press "No thanks", or leave it unanswered: nothing is collected.
  • Your browser's signal. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, statistics stay off without asking (you can still switch them on yourself).
  • Change your mind. Menu → Settings → Share anonymous usage statistics. Switching it off stops collection immediately.
  • Erase it. Menu → Settings → Erase my data deletes every session recorded under your browser's visitor number, then forgets the number.

There are no advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party trackers.

Online multiplayer

In an online room, the commander name you choose and your chat messages are relayed to the other players in that room. They are held in the server's memory while the room exists and are not written to disk or included in the statistics above.

Questions, or something wrong?

Open an issue on the project's source repository (linked from the About box in the game).
